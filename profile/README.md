# Jollir

**Better websites. Simpler operations. Practical software.**

Jollir is a founder-led digital consulting and software studio based in Lilongwe, Malawi. We help businesses improve their online presence, simplify everyday operations and build software that fits the way they work.

## How we can help

### Digital Consulting & Assessment

Understand what’s slowing your business down, compare the options and decide where to start.

### Websites & Online Presence

Build or improve a website so people can understand your business, find what they need and get in touch.

### Business Software & Integrations

Build software and connect compatible tools to reduce repetitive work, organise records and support everyday operations.

### Maintenance & Ongoing Improvement

Keep websites and applications working with updates, fixes and improvements within an agreed scope.

## How we work

Good solutions start with understanding the problem. We take time to understand your needs, assess the options and agree on a practical plan before implementation.

You can come to us for an assessment and recommendations on their own.
